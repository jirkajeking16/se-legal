Script Engine – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Script Engine is an internal tool operated by Misfits Media s.r.o. ("we", "us"). It helps us plan and improve the scripts for YouTube channels that we own or manage. This policy explains what data the tool accesses through Google and YouTube, and how that data is used.

Who can use it

Script Engine is not offered to the public. It is used only by our own team, with YouTube channels that we own or manage.

What data we access

When a channel owner connects a YouTube channel with Google sign-in, Script Engine requests read-only access through these Google scopes:

  • YouTube Analytics (read-only) (yt-analytics.readonly): per-video statistics such as views, watch time, average view duration, average percentage viewed, likes, comments, subscribers gained and audience-retention data.
  • YouTube (read-only) (youtube.readonly): the channel's name and ID, and the list of uploaded videos with their titles, publish dates, length and privacy status.

Script Engine cannot upload, edit, delete or comment on videos, and it cannot change any channel or account settings. It does not access email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How we use the data

We use this data only to see how our own videos performed. That lets us find which parts of a script kept viewers watching and which parts lost them, and improve future scripts for the same channels. Statistics may be analyzed with AI tools that we use for this purpose. The data is not used for advertising, profiling individual viewers or any other purpose.

The data we receive is aggregated channel and video statistics. It does not identify individual viewers.

Storage and security

Retrieved statistics and the Google sign-in tokens are stored on our own computers. Access is limited to our team, and tokens are kept in access-restricted files. We keep video statistics for as long as we need them to improve our scripts, and we delete them when they are no longer needed or when a channel is disconnected.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data obtained through Google APIs with third parties. The only exception is service providers that process it for us as described above, such as AI tools used to analyze our own statistics, or where the law requires it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Script Engine's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Script Engine uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can disconnect Script Engine at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. To ask us to delete data we stored for a channel, contact us at the address below.
